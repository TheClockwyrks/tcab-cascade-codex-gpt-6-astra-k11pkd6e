Cascade is a quiet game of Klondike with a spectacular finish. Settle into a deep green table, turn over a fresh deck, and find a little order in the shuffle. There is no clock to race and no score to chase.

Play three cards at a time from the stock, with the front card ready to move. Build descending columns in alternating colors, uncover the cards beneath, and save an empty space for a King. Drag a card or a whole run into place; double-click a ready card to send it home.

Your aim is to build four foundations, each running from Ace to King in one suit. Recycle the stock as often as you like, consider another route through the columns, or begin again with a new deal. Mouse and touch both feel at home at this table.

Place the last King and let go: all fifty-two cards leap from their foundations, bounce across the felt, and leave a tumbling trail of color behind them. When the last card drifts away, the painted table is yours to enjoy. One tap begins another game.
